ACCOUNT DELETION

Delete Account

FoFit includes an in-app account deletion path. This page explains how to start deletion and what is removed.

Last updated July 5, 2026

Delete in the app

  • Open FoFit and sign in to the account you want deleted.
  • Go to Settings > Profile & Account > Delete account.
  • Read the warning, type DELETE, and confirm.
  • FoFit signs you out after the backend confirms deletion.

If you cannot access the app

Email support@fofit.app from the email address on your FoFit account with the subject "Delete my FoFit account". Include the account email and any sign-in provider you used. We may ask for additional verification before deleting an account.

What deletion removes

  • Your FoFit auth account and user-scoped cloud data.
  • Workout plans, sessions, PRs, body metrics, goals, nutrition logs, saved foods, Cypher chat history, coach-memory notes, and community/profile data tied to your user id.
  • Uploaded media such as profile photos, progress photos, community post media, and reel media on a best-effort storage purge after account deletion.

What may remain briefly

  • Rolling backups until they expire.
  • Security, abuse-prevention, and diagnostic records that are no longer linked to your account where retention is required or operationally necessary.
  • Content already exported, cached, screenshotted, or stored outside FoFit by other people or third-party services.

App Review demo account

The shared App Review demo account is preserved so reviewers can test FoFit repeatedly. Use Sign out when finished. A fresh test account can still exercise the destructive deletion path.
