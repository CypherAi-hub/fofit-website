FOFIT HELP

Support

Need help with FoFit, account access, billing visibility, privacy, bugs, safety, or App Review? Contact support and include enough context to reproduce the issue.

Last updated July 5, 2026

Contact support

Email support@fofit.app. Include your account email, device model, iOS version, FoFit version/build, what you expected, what happened, and screenshots or screen recordings if they help.

What we can help with

  • Account access, sign-in, Sign in with Apple, demo account review questions, and profile setup.
  • Workout, nutrition, Cypher, media, community, reels, reports, blocks, mutes, permissions, and data sync issues.
  • Privacy questions, data export requests, correction requests, account deletion requests, and safety concerns.
  • Bug reports, broken links, missing exercise media, and App Store reviewer support.
