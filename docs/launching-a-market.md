# Launch a FoFit market

Website market settings are editorial configuration, not native feature flags, legal approval, billing entitlements, or store availability. US preserves the existing pre-release message; CA, GB, AU, BR, MX, FR, DE, JP and KR are planned. GLOBAL is the unknown-market fallback. No market is newly enabled by this change.

Before publishing market-specific content:

- Verify app availability, supported language/features and real onboarding with the same account/history. Website query parameters alone do not save preferences.
- Review and publish the relevant translations. Check exercise terminology, local foods, units, support and notification coverage in the app separately.
- Set only verified App Store/Play URLs; absent links stay null. Use billing-provider localized price strings in the app. The website has no international price schedule or exchange-rate fallback.
- Review terms/privacy, support language, age and consent requirements with the responsible reviewers. Existing English links remain until genuine replacement policies are approved; no GDPR/LGPD or other compliance claim is implied.
- Supply real licensed screenshots, creator/campaign codes and permissioned testimonials if available. Null configuration is preferable to fictional proof. Review consent before enabling analytics or conversion capture.
- Validate the complete install/account/purchase/support journey in that country, then deliberately change market stage and deploy the reviewed pages through the normal release process.

Brazil tomorrow is blocked by unreviewed Portuguese copy and unverified app/food/support/billing/policy readiness. Japan has the same gaps plus Japanese terminology and full text-layout review. This website foundation does not resolve those product or operational checks. Its English-only main marketing, policies/support, and release status remain the principal website limits.
